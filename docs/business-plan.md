# TrendFi Business Plan

## Executive Summary

TrendFi is a decentralized derivatives platform that transforms Google Trends data into tradable assets with leverage. By combining perpetual futures mechanics with sophisticated limit order books, we're creating a new asset class: trend derivatives.

## Problem Statement

**Current prediction markets are limited:**
- Binary outcomes only (yes/no questions)
- No leverage or sophisticated trading tools
- Limited to events with clear end dates
- Poor price discovery mechanisms

**Trend data is valuable but untapped:**
- Google Trends represents real-time public interest
- Trends have predictable patterns and volatility
- No existing platform for trading trend movements
- Massive market for trend speculation and hedging

## Solution

**TrendFi Platform:**
- **Perpetual Futures on Trends**: Trade long/short positions on Google Trends data with up to 10x leverage
- **Advanced Order Book**: Limit orders, market orders, and sophisticated trading tools
- **Real-time Oracle**: Cryptographically verified Google Trends data feeds
- **Risk Management**: Circuit breakers, liquidations, and position limits
- **Creator Economy**: Market creators earn fees based on trading volume

## Market Opportunity

**Target Market Size:**
- Global derivatives market: $1 quadrillion+
- Crypto derivatives: $3 trillion+ daily volume
- Prediction markets: $10 billion+ (growing rapidly)
- Digital advertising insights: $500 billion+

**Addressable Market:**
- Crypto traders seeking new volatility
- Marketing professionals hedging trend risk
- Speculators on viral content and news
- Data-driven investors

**Competitive Advantages:**
1. **First-mover** in trend derivatives
2. **Superior technology** to existing prediction markets
3. **Real asset class** vs binary gambling
4. **Professional trading features**

## Product Architecture

### Smart Contracts
- **TrendMarketV2**: Core perpetual futures engine
- **LimitOrderBook**: Advanced order matching
- **TrendOracle**: Verified data feeds
- **MarketFactory**: Market creation and management
- **RiskManager**: Position monitoring and liquidations

### Data Pipeline
```
Google Trends → Ingestion Job → Supabase → Oracle API → Smart Contracts
```

### Key Features
- **Leverage Trading**: Up to 10x leverage on trend movements
- **Funding Rates**: Balance long/short positions automatically
- **Limit Orders**: Professional order book with depth
- **Market Creation**: Anyone can create markets for new trends
- **Risk Controls**: Circuit breakers and liquidation mechanisms

## Business Model

### Revenue Streams

**1. Trading Fees**
- **Base Fee**: 0.5% on all position openings/closings
- **Creator Fees**: 0.05%-0.95% based on market volume
- **Treasury Fees**: 20% of all fees go to protocol treasury

**2. Market Creation Fees**
- **Creation Fee**: 0.01 ETH to create new markets
- **Template Fees**: Custom market templates with premium features

**3. Advanced Features**
- **API Access**: Premium data feeds for institutions
- **Analytics Tools**: Advanced trend analysis and insights
- **Professional Features**: High-frequency trading tools

### Fee Structure
```
Position Opening: 0.5% base fee + creator fee
Position Closing: 0.5% base fee + creator fee (on profits only)
Market Creation: 0.01 ETH fixed fee
Treasury Split: 20% of all fees
```
